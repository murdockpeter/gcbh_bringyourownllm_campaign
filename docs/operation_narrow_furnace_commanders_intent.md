# Operation Narrow Furnace — Commander's Intent

**Start:** 22 May 2026, 16:30 Zulu  
**Area:** Strait of Hormuz  
**Mission type:** Corridor opening and protected westbound transit

## Situation

Operation Resolute Passage destroyed RED's four MiG-29s and two Su-24Ms but did not open the corridor. Wildcat 2 was destroyed by Khanjar's naval gunfire, Wildcat 1 returned damaged, and the convoy and coastal network remained intact. Six apparent RED surface losses were route-convergence artifacts and are not campaign losses.

The convoy has regrouped east of Hormuz. BLUE holds temporary air superiority, but Tiger 1/2 and Viper 1/2 are recovering after heavy AIM-120 expenditure. Tiger 3/4 provide the fresh CAP. RED has dispersed all nine surface combatants, retains an intact coastal kill chain, and has moved Shahin 1/2 to Bandar Abbas as a staged reserve.

## Mission

Escort at least five of six auxiliaries west through Hormuz, suppress at least three of the five designated corridor-control nodes, and neutralize Khanjar and Falakhon without expanding the operation beyond the authorized corridor.

## Purpose

Deliver the fuel, missile-defense reloads, repair modules, and medical stores required to sustain the coalition force inside the Gulf while demonstrating that the exclusion zone cannot close an internationally used passage.

## Key tasks

1. Lead the transit with Devastator and Dextrous; do not allow faster vessels to overrun the MCM lanes.
2. Keep all six auxiliaries in their assigned two-column formation and inside the escort screen.
3. Maintain Tiger 3/4 between the convoy and northern air approaches.
4. Use Broadarrow and Sentinel to build the surface and air picture before committing strike aircraft.
5. Neutralize Khanjar and Falakhon before Lancer crosses their effective gun envelope.
6. Hold Lancer at the standoff initial points until named coastal targets are positively designated.
7. Suppress at least three of Bastion 1–3, S-300 Site North, and Coastwatch 1.
8. Preserve weapons for the western missile ambush; the dispersed reserve is not required to be destroyed.

## Priorities

1. Convoy survival
2. Air and missile defense
3. Positive contact identification
4. Eastern-picket neutralization
5. Limited coastal suppression
6. Orderly westbound passage

## End state

- At least five auxiliaries remain operational and continue west.
- Khanjar and Falakhon can no longer threaten the transit.
- At least three named coastal nodes are destroyed or disabled.
- BLUE retains a functioning escort screen and viable CAP.
- Bandar Abbas and the Shahin reserve have not been attacked absent a new hostile act and expanded authority.
- No loss is credited without weapon attribution or corroborating simulation evidence.

## Rules of engagement

- Preplanned shore attack is limited to Bastion 1, Bastion 2, Bastion 3, S-300 Site North, and Coastwatch 1.
- Pantsirs may be engaged in self-defense or while directly protecting an authorized target.
- Bandar Abbas Strip and Shahin 1/2 are not preauthorized targets.
- Lancer has no unrestricted automatic attack task; the player designates targets.
- Other RED surface vessels may be engaged when hostile intent is established or in defense of the convoy.

## Commander's critical decisions

- **Commit Lancer:** after Khanjar and Falakhon are neutralized or their gun envelope is safely bypassed.
- **Commit the convoy into the narrows:** after a usable coastal-threat picture exists and the escort screen is aligned.
- **Divert from the planned lane:** if a route is obstructed, a mine threat is confirmed, or escort cohesion is lost.
- **Abort coastal attack:** if positive identification is lost, weapons would leave the authorized target set, or the CAP cannot protect the package.
- **Withdraw or hold:** if two auxiliaries are lost, both fresh CAP aircraft become unavailable, or the formation becomes unable to defend against the western ambush.

## Campaign continuity controls

- Wildcat 2 and Fulcrum 1–4/Fencer 1–2 are excluded as confirmed losses.
- Wildcat 1 remains at Al Dhafra with 62% campaign structural integrity.
- Tiger 1/2, Viper 1/2, and Shahin 1/2 are physically staged, not spawned airborne.
- IRIS Sahand, Dagger, Scimitar, Zolfaghar, Tsunami 1, and Tsunami 2 remain present because their previous losses were noncanonical.
- RED surface routes use unique turn points and passed both coastline and GCBH safe-water validation.
