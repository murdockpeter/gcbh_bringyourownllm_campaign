Rapid decision gates

Before every major commitment, ask four things.

If any answer is unclear, pause long enough to rebuild the picture or choose the lower-risk action.

01

What must be protected?

Name the threatened logistics ship, sensor, defender, or recovery route.

02

What is the actual threat?

Separate inbound weapon, shooter, cueing node, and merely visible contact.

03

What is the least-cost answer?

Choose the right domain, weapon, timing, and supporting cover.

04

What happens after release?

Check screen integrity, fuel, magazine depth, egress, and campaign value.
