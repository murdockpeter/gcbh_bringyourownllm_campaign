03 · Commander’s decision aid

Commit combat power in the right order.

01

Picture before weapons

  • Keep Sentinel broad and survivable.
  • Use Broadarrow/Trident to classify surface contacts.
  • Do not spend long-range weapons on ambiguous tracks.
02

Defend in layers

  • Chosin and Burke provide depth.
  • Mason/Hudner reinforce threatened axes.
  • Diamond and the close screen protect the convoy, not the horizon.
03

Kill shooters first

  • Inbound weapons outrank launch platforms.
  • Fencers and missile boats outrank low-threat contacts.
  • Strike Bastion/Coastwatch when classification and access permit.
04

Keep the lane coherent

  • Devastator/Dextrous lead; auxiliaries follow.
  • Avoid formation compression at the narrows.
  • Do not let escorts chase contacts away from sealift.
05

Preserve recovery paths

  • Tiger flight recovers to the carrier east.
  • Land-based aircraft recover to Al Dhafra.
  • Shell 1 is an endurance tool, not a forward scout.
!

Manual-air-control caveat

Player-controlled aircraft do not retain AutoAttack. Assign targets, weapon release, routing, and recovery deliberately. A quiet aircraft may be awaiting player action—not malfunctioning.
